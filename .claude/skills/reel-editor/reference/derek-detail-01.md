## Measured details

- **Caption sizes at 1080 wide:**
  - Connector words in light italic: 75–97 px.
  - Regular phrases: ~105 px.
  - Bold keywords: 112–142 px.
  - Extrabold-italic phrases: 110–120 px.
  - The hero word "paint": ~250 px.
  - Shadow: soft and dark at about 45%. Text is white only.
- **Sweep:** a teal (#46CDEB) → violet (#A573FF) band runs left to right over the keyword
  in about 0.5 s and settles on the last letters ("Automa**tic**", "**d**irty",
  "YOUR PA**INT**").
- **Word entry:** slides in from the left with horizontal motion blur over about 0.15 s.
  The hero word pops in: it starts big and blurred, then snaps to size.
- **Page exit:** fade out over about 0.15 s, which differs from ref 01's hard swap.
- **PiP:** about 66% of the frame width × 27% of the height, centred at x 0.5 and spanning y 0.30–0.57.
  Hard edges, no border. It appears on a cut and fades out.
- **Spin at 16.75:** the old shot starts rotating, then the new shot arrives rotated
  about 20° with zoom and rotational blur. It settles in about 5 frames at 30 fps.
- **No punch-in zooms.** The talking head is a wide full-body shot that walks toward
  the camera and gestures. The CTA is cut across two or three different positions.
